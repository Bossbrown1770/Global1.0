Returns Policy

Return Process

1

Initiate Return

Contact our support team within 30 days of delivery to initiate a return request. Provide your tracking ID and reason for return.

2

Receive Return Label

We will email you a prepaid return shipping label within 24 hours of your request.

3

Ship the Package

Pack the item securely, attach the return label, and drop it off at any authorized shipping location.

4

Inspection & Refund

Once we receive and inspect the item, your refund will be processed within 5-7 business days.

Return Conditions

  • Items must be returned within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items must be in original condition with all packaging and tags.
  • Perishable goods, custom orders, and hazardous materials cannot be returned.
  • Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items.
  • For change-of-mind returns, a restocking fee of 10% may apply.
